About

I'm a senior majoring in Earth Science and Geography. I like questions where the physical world and human decisions meet: where water goes, who gets it, and what places are called and why.

I spent two semesters in Poland on a bilateral exchange program. Studying in a second academic system gave me a new set of landscapes to learn from, and a clearer sense of what I'd like to do next.

Selected work

  • The Water Scarcity of Kazakhstan: Data and Projections

    Presentation

    A data-driven look at water stress in Kazakhstan, covering the Aral Sea, transboundary rivers, irrigated agriculture, and groundwater.

  • Classification of Neotoponyms in Akmola Region

    Mind map and research notes

    A bilingual (Russian and English) classification of newly created place names, built to show how new names fall into patterns.

  • New Directions in Teaching Toponymy

    Lecture for geography lessons

    A lecture on bringing place-name study into school geography classes, with ideas for making it concrete for students.

  • Forming a Nature-Use Structure

    Lecture

    How to plan the use of land and resources around the features of the local geosystem, rather than against them.
